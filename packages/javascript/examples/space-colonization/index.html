PROCEDURAL / GROWTH

Space Colonization

Tips grow toward the nearest unconsumed source; a tip that reaches a source consumes it and branches into rotated offsets. The teal links are the grown network; the coral tips are the active front. Branches sets how many new tips each reached source spawns; Spread sets how wide they fan out.

Edit the seed, the target grid, or the growth density in sketch.js. The step is a single bounded space-colonization operation; the tips, segments and consumed flags are its retained output.
