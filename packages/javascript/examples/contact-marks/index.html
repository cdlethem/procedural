Contact marks

Ten query segments run from authored start points toward the center of the canvas; each one is connected to its nearest contact point on a four-edge obstacle quadrilateral, computed with exact rational segment arithmetic.

Preparing contacts…

The thin lines are the queries, the heavy dark quadrilateral is the obstacle, and each colored connector ends at the nearest contact point. Edit the composition in contact-marks.js: the start points, obstacle corners, and palettes are authored piece settings, and the obstacle list is the substitution point for retained polygon edges or another line drawing.
