Body marks

Twelve retained heads step forward one tick per frame through a seeded field while each body's spine retraces backward from the new head, drawn as a tapered body outline or a bare centerline.

Preparing bodies…

Edit the composition in body-marks.js: the head layout, field scale, step distance, and spine length rebuild the traced geometry each tick; the sketch's body/centerline draw modes are the place to invent another treatment of the same spines. These values describe this piece.
