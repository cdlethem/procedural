Relief marks

A structural recreation of Manolo ide's momito. A seeded quadrant partition creates surface centres, Delaunay triangulation establishes their facets, and a separate Java-compatible stream decides which centres rise as fine white spikes.

Building the relief…

C changes the relief color; H toggles low and tall faces; R makes a new seed (a fresh partition, mesh, and spike selection); 0 restores seed 42; S saves the cached frame without redraw. This is a structural recreation: the Java composition's geometry and policy are ported, not an exact browser-raster claim.
