Blur marks

Filter one semi-transparent artwork layer three ways (isotropic soft, horizontal streak, vertical streak) with a normalized separable convolution, optionally crossfade left-to-right back toward the sharp original, then composite the result over a striped ground.

Preparing layers…

Edit the composition in blur-marks.js: the two triangular kernel radii (soft vs. directional) are fixed at construction and produce three retained blurred layers up front; the sketch's mode/blend controls only select and recompute the crossfade/composite from those retained layers. The ground and artwork rasters are drawn once in sketch.js and handed to the composition as plain pixel buffers, keeping the blend/composite logic testable without a canvas. These values describe this piece.
