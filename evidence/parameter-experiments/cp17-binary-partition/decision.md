## Limits and next decision

The two source notes have no parameter experiments. Their integer-cell layout mechanics,
not their decorative constants, motivate the prototype. The independent portable RNG,
all-interior cut mapping and explicit attempt consumption intentionally differ from
Processing source streams. Source extent-two cut behavior is not silently inherited.

Positive signed-int cell extents and attempt representation ceilings are engineering
domains, not measured resource limits. No guessed maximum runtime or memory claim follows.
The private ordered ArrayList prototype has O(attempts * liveLeaves) worst-case work and
O(liveLeaves) retained memory. Public implementation still requires representative-range
and unsaturated stress measurements, shared JSON fixtures and a reviewed catalog contract.

Proceed to a binary integer-cell partition operation; leave world translation/scaling and
cell decoration in ordinary sketch drawing. Do not merge unequal-quadrant subdivision,
biased dropping, region decoration or barab's connector/halo algorithms into it.
