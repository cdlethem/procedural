# Additional CP7 neighbours: annular solids and irregular axial bands

Root inspected the two remaining records assigned to the provisional `mesh.revolve`
family, including their full reports and pinned source. Hash bindings and the complete
six-source inspection scope are in
[the root source review](../../evidence/investigations/cp7-profile-source-review.json).
This supplements the first bounded audit; it does not change its historical scope.

`2017/Generativos/Circo#2` supplies positive band heights accumulated along Z, with a
constant ring radius. Its base quads wind inward. The second coincident overlay, palette
progression around each band, random bisection and dramatic camera are separate work.
The proposed profile generator can represent its geometric open side surface after a
deliberate outward-winding choice; it does not implement the entire `polarRing` helper.
This also supports retaining both axial-band and angular-cell identities for styling.
No measured parameter variants appear in its note.

`2017/Generativos/aros#0` is a valuable distinct topology task: a washer with inner and
outer walls. The source additionally emits horizontal radial faces at every axial
subdivision, duplicating interior faces between neighbouring layers. It winds the inner
wall in the same direction as the outer wall. After halving `h`, it uses `hh=h/sub`,
therefore covering Z from `-h` to zero, despite the note's `[-h,h]` description. Its
animation consumes wall-clock time and its note has no parameter experiments. These
facts prevent an unqualified merge into a clean closed-profile mesh operation.

A washer cannot be represented by a single-valued positive radius versus strictly
increasing axial position. Extending the first operation to a closed arbitrary meridian
would add closed-profile orientation, same-height edges, inner walls, genus-one topology,
and self-intersection/degeneracy questions. That may be worthwhile, but it is a separate
architectural comparison, not an invisible extension of the current cylinder/waist/pole
investigation. Keep annular solids in the capability backlog; do not claim CP7 covers
them or reject the family. The first comparison should establish a useful form-building
workflow before deciding whether the broader profile mode earns that extra surface.

No ledger dispositions were changed. In particular, the old prueba4 provisional merge
reason says adjacent rings are connected; the exact source contradicts that reading.
Any CP7 admission must reopen or explicitly supersede that equivalence claim rather than
use its provisional label as authority.
