## Required composition

seed42,290 replacements, selectionFraction0.5. Retain871 leaves and their centers/minimum spans.

artistic range or latency claim.

The PDE draws each canonical face's first two vertices at z=4 and third at z=0, plus two
triangles along the elevated edge down to0. Keep this nine-vertex pattern visible in an
editable drawing helper. Native P3D computes its flat face shading. Preserve the source's
open relief character; do not introduce a closed extrusion or an unseen generic mesh API.

Use one palette colour per frame, initially the source palette's dark green0x366A51. Keep

Use a960×960 P3D density1 canvas, centered view, explicit orthographic projection and fixed
