# CP9 retained Delaunay dependency admitted for contract preparation

Root admits `topology.delaunay-2d` as an independently specified dependency of
FacetMarks. This approves the computation boundary, not a public signature,
production implementation, recommended range, or delivered capability.

The operation consumes supplied finite planar sites and an explicit work budget.
It returns retained canonical vertices, both input/vertex identity mappings,
positively oriented indexed faces, and unique edges with incident-face indices.
Duplicate coordinates collapse exactly. Small/all-collinear inputs retain sites
and mappings with zero faces and edges. No RNG, renderer, palette, scatter,
clipping, source-object mutation or third-party compatibility is included.

The rationale and meaningful editing tasks are in `cp9-facet-marks-direction.md`;
the selected data/degeneracy boundary is in `cp9-boundary-decisions.md`. Root read
the decisive datata/lightcity notes and source calls, the three-port degeneracy
audit, the exact-predicate investigation and the topology prototype. Root also
independently checked the port's x-only comparator/shared-vertex defect and
exhaustively tested the cocircular tie rule on 18,379 convex triangulations.

The small exact prototype passed 21 cases, including three duplicate-input
permutations, hull/internal-edge insertion and both strict and tie flips. Its
checker uses an independent lifted determinant and independently constructed
hull/supporting lines. That evidence supports the chosen hull-fan/insertion/flip
strategy; it does not establish production performance or all-binary64 behavior.

No ordinary candidate is moved into this new cluster. The datata triangulator
and lightcity imported call motivate the geometric dependency while remaining
in their existing candidate accounting. The new canonical ownership, exact
predicate semantics, mappings, edges and tie policy are deliberate design
differences. The full `geometry.delaunay` family has not been exhaustively merged,
and no Voronoi family rejection follows from this admission.

Contract preparation must specify exact validation/error precedence, count
ceilings, work accounting, observable traversal and queue behavior, output
schemas, ownership/access methods and fixtures. Representative Java workloads
and stress inputs remain mandatory before implementation acceptance. The
private prototype's 64-site limit and 100,000 edge examinations are diagnostic
guards, not proposed library limits. Its repeated full edge-map rebuilding must
not become an unmeasured production algorithm.

Root owns the review under the maintainer's paused-Sol sprint policy. The
architecture prerequisite checker is a structural check; scoped implementation,
native drawing, visual transfer and packaging acceptance remain later gates.
