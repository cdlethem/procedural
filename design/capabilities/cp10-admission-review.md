# CP10 target-driven spring batch admitted for contract preparation

Root admits `motion.target-springs-2d` as a computation extracted from
`2018/Generativos/araniaaas#1`. This approves the operation boundary for contract
preparation, not a frozen public signature, implementation or shipped capability.
The [artist direction](cp10-spring-marks-direction.md) and
[state decision](cp10-state-boundary-decision.md) describe its task and tradeoffs.

The operation owns a fixed-size collection of independent positions and velocities.
An explicit step consumes supplied targets and advances each item by target-directed
acceleration, position advancement using that updated velocity, then velocity
retention. The native batch retains current state for drawing and further stepping.
It must provide detached observations and complete-batch failure atomicity without
allocating a new history every tick. There is no implicit time, RNG, global pointer,
renderer, inter-site force, topology update or guaranteed settling.

The source's target-return lerp and pointer falloff remain example target policies.
Random initialization of per-site decay, global Processing state and binary32
compatibility remain outside the selected portable computation. Source aliases to
vectors/triangles are not retained; initial indexed geometry may be drawn with
current positions as a deliberate deformation transfer. This does not claim those
positions still form a Delaunay triangulation.

This is a component extraction, not a whole-helper merge. The authored ledger
moves only araniaaas#1 from the provisional motion.spring-drift family into the
new operation candidate and accounts for its remaining components. Its source
identity/text/hash are preserved. Other motion mechanisms and other candidates
remain unchanged; sparse evidence is not a physics-family rejection.

Root read the complete report and both source tabs, reviewed the related stateful
counterexamples, and ran the original Point recurrence headlessly. Its initialized
no-input state remains exactly still; the earlier autonomous-drift description is
rejected. The registered coefficient investigation then completed seven native
JAVA2D cases, all42 required frames and all121 states per case. Root inspected all31
distinct images. Strength and retention each produced distinct controlled effects;
zero retention still moved because damping follows position advancement. Full
retention oscillated through the observation window. The
[parameter decision](../../evidence/parameter-experiments/cp10-spring-response/decision.md)
retains both controls without recommending defaults or continuous artistic ranges.

A batch is warranted by the continuing artist task: retain a moving arrangement,
retarget it, pause/step/replay and restyle it without advancing. The kernel must
remove state/ownership and stepping work from the sketch. It must not become a
single arithmetic wrapper or an opaque scene function. The small source recurrence
is sufficient motivation for investigation; its frequency alone does not establish
value. A complete SpringMarks workflow must still prove that the retained abstraction
is useful with independent marks and fixed indexed edges.

Contract preparation now resolves exact numeric domains, update/validation order,
carrier rules, safe sizes, atomic arithmetic failure, detached access/export,
coefficient ownership and reset/replay. Required fixtures include equilibrium,
explicit impulses and target sequences, damping order, nonzero starting state,
late invalid inputs and overflow, and atomic access buffers. Mathematical numeric
bounds must be justified separately from the measured illustrative values.

Root reviews this boundary under the maintainer's paused-Sol sprint policy. The
structural eligibility checker cannot establish semantic correctness or replace
future native implementation, performance, visual workflow and package acceptance.
