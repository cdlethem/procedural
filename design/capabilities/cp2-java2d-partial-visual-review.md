# Public PathMarks partial visual observations

Root inspected marks, trace and long-marks from the second, failed desktop execution at
`.work/reproductions/cp2-public-pde-double-literals/`. These images are observations from
that attempt; they do not make the attempt pass or establish the later distance/save states.

The trace has continuous thin curves, broad turns, merged routes and paths leaving the
canvas. Marks attach in coherent perpendicular bands along the same movement. Length24
widens those bands while retaining the curves; crossing and converging paths visibly
overlap. The images express the intended distinction between movement and mark treatment.

Direct RGB comparison against the corresponding accepted private design images in
`.work/experiments/cp2-path-choice/` gave exact decoded equality (RGB MAE0) for all three.
PNG container hashes differ; no byte-identity claim follows. Observed public PNG hashes:

- marks: `320f2108ff170bb857d72883ebf34ed05e4cc41bf015a732ee860f39594145d4`
