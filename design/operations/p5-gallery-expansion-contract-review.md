# p5 expansion contract review

The three catalog entries are normative. Root owns their semantics and approves
implementation only against the frozen entries and analytical shared fixtures.
They add independently designed computations under the maintainer's explicit scope.

The independent challenge confirms that nearest-site cells differ from Delaunay
triangles, contour extraction differs from field integration, and explicit polyline
sampling must preserve corners/open endpoints rather than replace the closed spline.
Root adopts stable duplicate ownership, exact equality rules, no implicit tolerance
in topology, deterministic edge ordering and explicit resource budgets. Floating
geometry is binary64; selected analytical fixture coordinates compare exactly, while
general geometric properties use the field tolerances recorded in the contracts.

Gallery expansion changes the Studio binding digest. Integration must preserve the
previous v3 binding through an explicit additive migration and reject unknown digests.
Study previews must come from actual adapters, and package examples remain editable.
Acceptance is separate from this design approval and is recorded after execution.

Root resolved two numerical review findings before final acceptance: resampling count
is limited by the p5 array-length representation (4294967295), and rounded subnormal
closed samples may repeat the seam. Shared fixtures distinguish both cases. Marching
squares returns exact edge endpoints for interpolation fractions zero and one and
validates supplied scalar data before reporting an unsafe work descriptor.
