# Space-colonization step contract review

Root freezes the `growth.space-colonization-step-2d` contract for the third
I-family slice. The family design (`.work/expansion-full/i-family-design.md`,
row 59) and this review record the frozen choices before implementation; the
shared exact-json fixtures contain only exact outputs and error codes, none
generated by the implementation under test.

Resolved choices:
- One bounded operation, `spaceColonizationStep2D`, a pure function of its input
  (tips, sources, the consumed flags, and the growth parameters) that performs a
  single growth step and returns the new tips, the segments grown this step, and
  the updated consumed flags. No persistent state; the study loops the step to
  grow the retained network.
- Direct nearest-unconsumed-source aim (no momentum): each tip aims straight at
  the nearest not-yet-consumed source by squared Euclidean distance, ties broken
  by the lower source index. This is the frozen first-slice aim; steering with an
  inherited direction is a documented follow-up.
- Reach and branch in the same step: a tip within `reach` of its nearest source
  consumes that source (a consumed source is never regrown — node identity),
  emits the segment from the tip to the source, and is replaced by `branches`
  new tips placed at the source offset by `step` in the growth direction rotated
  by evenly spaced angles centered on it (so the branches separate). A tip
  beyond `reach` grows by `step` along the direction to its source and emits the
  segment to the new position. A tip with no unconsumed source is removed
  (growth termination); it emits no segment and adds no tip.
- Branch angle law: for `branches >= 2`, the k-th new tip (k from 0) is rotated
  by `(k - (branches-1)/2) * (2*branchAngle/(branches-1))` around the incoming
  growth direction; for `branches == 1`, the single new tip keeps the incoming
  direction (rotation 0). The incoming direction is the unit vector from the tip
  to the reached source; the new tip is `source + step * rotated`.
- Output size cap: the returned tips are capped at a frozen maximum; when the
  cap would be exceeded, the excess new tips (in emission order) are dropped and
  counted in `dropped`, so the output never grows without bound.
- Work is `tips.length * sources.length` (the nearest query), in exact integers,
  precharged before the loop; a breach is `WORK_LIMIT` and the call is atomic.

Fixtures distinguish the straight growth toward a nearer source, the reach and
consume with two-way branch separation, the no-source removal (a tip with all
sources consumed), the tie broken by source index, the single-branch passthrough,
the size-cap drop, and the INVALID_INPUT/WORK_LIMIT carriers. This contract
freeze permits implementation; it is not native, artist recreation or
target-support acceptance.
