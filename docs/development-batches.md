# Development batch entry point

The original Java breadth and recreation batches are complete within their recorded scopes.
Historical assignments remain in Git and their acceptance records remain under `evidence/`.
Do not resume them as an active queue.

Use [PROJECT_STATE](../PROJECT_STATE.md) for current work, [the roadmap](roadmap.md) for
remaining product workstreams, and [agent briefs](agent-briefs.md) for bounded ownership
and handoff requirements. Sustainable execution and review policy live in [AGENTS](../AGENTS.md).

For further capability work, root selects the artist task and evaluates marginal
[recreation coverage](recreation-coverage.md) before admitting an operation. New work is
not justified by an old checkpoint's “next” paragraph or by available worker capacity.
