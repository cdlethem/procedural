# Expanding what artists can make with p5.js

Research date: 17 September 2026. This is a capability research and delivery plan, not an API admission or implementation acceptance. The requested direction is **new p5.js capabilities**. Java, Python and Android parity do not gate this work.

The strongest next step is to combine better complete compositions with a few substantial new computations: interacting agents, non-overlapping thick paths, elastic growth, image-driven marks, and browser-native materials and media. Adding another catalog of isolated geometric demonstrations would leave much of the visual gap intact.

The [local image corpus](../.work/external-art-corpus/index.html) contains **a collection-led selection across 24 primary artist/studio groups** (final counts and coverage denominators are in the [collection report](external-art-corpus.md)). A record can represent a series or selected frame pair; it is not necessarily one independently designed algorithm. The [manifest](../evidence/external-art/2026-09/corpus.json) links each selection to its artist, source page, technique evidence, limitations and package comparison. The [source screen](../evidence/external-art/2026-09/source-screen.json) accounts for the thread's 83 extracted outbound references, including failures and references not selected. The two specifically requested sites anchor the selection: [Joshua Davis](https://joshuadavis.com/) and the [REAS index](https://index.reas.com/), supplemented by Reas's directly linked thematic essays.

## What the collection establishes

This is a purposive, collection-led corpus chosen for computational diversity. The selection begins with full accessible project/series indexes, follows collection subpages and pagination, then retains representative output variants and process views. Artist-wide image quotas are not used. Each index has a separate denominator; an artwork, a series, a Flickr album and an archive post are not interchangeable units. It is not a random sample or an assertion that private, inaccessible or unindexed work has been exhausted. The 2018 [Reddit thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/generative/comments/9opi59/cool_generative_artists_and_their_websites/) supplies a discovery network, not technical authority. Current project pages, artist explanations, code and institutional artwork records supply the evidence. Newer projects are included; the corpus is not restricted to art made in 2018.

Successfully collected raster images were decoded and all collected assets were hash-deduplicated; SVGs were XML-validated. Visual review has a narrower, explicitly recorded scope: portfolio overviews and decisive reference details, with source-level attribution checks and recorded exclusions. Root reviewed a representative image for each of Davis’s 216 accessible image-bearing projects and all 554 Reas catalogue records, plus selected other artist overviews and decisive details. This is not a claim that every selected variant received full-resolution inspection. Download receipts retain dimensions, byte counts, hashes, final URLs and retrieval times. Downloaded bytes and contact sheets stay in `.work/`; the tracked manifest and collection census identify both image URLs and stable source pages for recollection. Some CDN URLs expire and must be refreshed from the linked primary page. Gallery derivatives are navigation aids and do not replace originals. Artwork and photography remain credited reference material; no redistribution or model-training license is established.

An image can show branching, folding or interference without establishing the mechanism that produced it. The manifest distinguishes artist-described techniques, source-backed algorithms and visual inference. Animation stills do not validate movement, causal audio mapping, feedback, real-time performance, interactivity or fabrication. Exact artist seeds, algorithms, assets and artistic decisions are usually unavailable. **None of the new external artworks is a demonstrated Procedurals recreation.**

Collection limitations are explicit. Instagram shells and inaccessible archives did not yield useful artwork evidence. Some formerly useful URLs now redirect, park or show construction pages. Accessible landing pages are not equivalent to assessed oeuvres. Historical computer art, non-Western traditions, hand-coded typography, shader demoscene, sound, installation and fabrication cannot be exhausted by this thread. The family map below keeps further research visible, including families with no collected decisive sample.

## The actual starting point

The research began at `c7071577110b271c7a1a295d93729dbf3b737bd0` with 64 accepted p5 operations. Before this report was completed, the separate [survey-coverage batch](../evidence/coverage/batch1/integration-review.json) accepted grain, sampled point displacement and octave gradient noise. The [current inventory](external-art-current-capabilities.md) therefore covers **67 conformant cores, 67 scoped native operations, 106 editable package workflows, 90 browser studies and four scoped technique attestations**. All 67 contract hashes were reconciled. These are different denominators, not additive capability counts. This research does not reaccept the concurrent batch or infer any new artist recreations from it.

| Existing computation | What can already be reused | Boundary relevant to the new images |
|---|---|---|
| Placement and subdivision | Grids, Poisson points, greedy circle/convex-polygon filters, rectangle packing, binary/quadrant cuts, adjacency tile collapse | Greedy acceptance is not arbitrary non-convex packing or a global constraint solver; tile collapse has no general backtracking |
| Fields and paths | Seeded 2D/3D gradient noise and octave sums, sampled point displacement, scalar-grid curl, RK4 vector-grid tracing, gradient/noise-band traces, grid cost paths | A field tracer is not a fluid simulation, sensor-motor agent, collision-free thick path or adaptive integrator |
| Planar geometry | Voronoi, Delaunay, Lloyd relaxation, contours, hulls, simple-polygon triangulation and segment clipping, resampling, smoothing, offsets | Rectangle-clipped Voronoi, simple polygons and route offsets do not provide region Boolean operations, holes, robust ribbon unions or self-intersection repair |
| Rule systems | Parallel token rewriting, turtle interpretation, elementary and life-like cellular automata, bounded branch trees | No general stochastic/context-sensitive grammar, interacting flock, growth/remeshing solver or semantic city generator |
| Simulation | Gray–Scott concentration step, damped wave grid step, independent target springs | Real useful simulations already exist. They do not imply multiscale Turing models, elastic connected bodies, gravity/collisions or incompressible flow |
| Raster/color | Seeded RGB/alpha grain, convolution, blur, remapping, binary morphology, distance transform, dithering, quantization, masks, crossfade, RGB/Oklab ramps | No spatially connected image segmentation or k-means clustering; no pixel sorting, optical flow, pigment transport or reusable temporal framebuffer system |
| Mesh generation | Extrusions, annular solids, radial profiles, Loop subdivision and transported ribbons | Indexed geometry is not a complete mesh material/UV/rendering system, implicit solid model or 3D growth engine |
| App experience | Layer placement, palettes, controls, seed edits, source editing, reset/reload/save, storage | These app conveniences do not constitute package-level portable graphs, reusable asset pipelines or acceptance of arbitrary generated code |

Root inspected all 60 tenfold gallery baselines used in the original comparison. The three newly accepted native studies are separate scoped additions and do not replace that visual comparison. They are useful demonstrations of geometry, rules and transformations, but most use sparse marks, simple fills and a shared visual treatment. Compared with the references, the major compositional differences include many scales in one image, broad and fine marks together, accumulated history, shaped negative space, dense local structure, material variation and coherent interaction. Those observations suggest better examples and controls as well as missing algorithms; they do not invalidate the existing scoped checks.

## Lessons from the anchor artists

Davis offers several distinct tasks. *Organized Disruption* combines circle packing with weighted form choices and proximity responses; *Orbit of Influence* explicitly uses p5.js and SVG motifs moving around invisible planets. *NikeLab / Tennis Deconstructed* maps recorded match audio into motifs and color. *Swarm Vertex Textures* and *Run Run Blood* extend behavior into texture sampling and 3D presentation. Existing placement, palette and raster operations are useful ingredients, while motif assets, signal mappings, thickness and rendering need explicit treatment. The source explanations do not justify attributing every image to one general “Davis algorithm.” [Organized Disruption](https://joshuadavis.com/ArtBlocks-Organized-Disruption/), [Orbit of Influence](https://joshuadavis.com/fx-hash-Orbit-of-Influence/), [Tennis Deconstructed](https://joshuadavis.com/NikeLab-21-Mercer-USOpen-Tennis-Deconstructed/).

Reas's *Process 18* makes persistent contact relationships visible as quadrilaterals that gain and lose opacity. *Path*, *Tissue*, *MicroImage* and *Phototaxis* draw with simple sensor-motor machines and accumulated trajectories. A static vector field can resemble some traces while omitting the defining interaction. The package needs a clear distinction between integrating through a field, stepping an agent, finding neighboring agents and maintaining a pair's history. [Process](https://reas.com/process/), [MicroImage](https://reas.com/microimage/).

Reas also prevents a narrow definition of the research. *Atomism* translates generated imagery into lines and signals; *METACENTURY* explores geometric arrangement and optical effects; *Compressed Cinema* relies on trained generative models. Elementary shape drawing, arbitrary signal transformation and learned synthesis demand different boundaries. The last should remain an explicitly external model/asset workflow unless separately selected for development. [Atomism](https://reas.com/atomism/), [CENTURY](https://reas.com/century/), [Compressed Cinema](https://reas.com/compressed-cinema/).

## Ordered delivery plan

The order below favors reusable computations that help multiple visually different tasks. It is a reasoned priority, not a frequency score. Working names describe candidate boundaries; implementation must still follow a root-reviewed API design and operation contract. A batch may end with an example and no new public function.

### A. Complete compositions and motif assets — first

**Artist task:** turn a sparse arrangement into a deliberate poster, patterned panel or orbital brush drawing, then replace its marks without rewriting layout. Start from Davis's motif work and Reas's geometric compositions. **Computation removed:** initially existing placement, sampling, transforms and palette operations; new asset handling only where loading, path interpretation or bounds are repetitive. **Alternative:** ordinary p5 drawing is the right solution for a few simple shapes; an “artist-style generator” is not a useful boundary.

**Inputs/output:** caller-supplied motif paths or a known SVG subset, anchors, placement records and style data; retain reusable transforms and source geometry. Keep asset loading outside pure geometry. **Meaningful edits:** change the motif set, occupancy, scale hierarchy, crop and palette independently. **Transfer:** botanical ornaments to abstract letter-like emblems. **Acceptance:** three original composed studies with visibly different density/structure, a motif substitution, reset/reload/save and retained geometry reused with another mark. Source-owned motifs should be replaced by original or clearly licensed assets. No artist recreation claim follows automatically.

### B. Neighborhoods, agent steps and contact history — first algorithm batch

**Artist task:** grow a drawing from elements responding to each other, with editable attraction, avoidance and relationship duration. Reas *Process 18*, *Tissue*, LIA and Hodgin motivate related but different rules. **Computation removed:** deterministic neighborhood enumeration and synchronous stepping; stable pair IDs and state history where relationship drawing needs them. **Alternative:** all-pairs example code is useful as a small correctness oracle, but artists should not have to hide an agent engine in a callback.

**Inputs/output:** explicit positions, headings/velocities, radii or sensing distances and timestep; return neighbor/contact data and detached next state. Sensor-motor wiring, flock steering and gravity should not collapse into one opaque mode switch. **Edits:** contact radius versus opacity persistence; sensor angle versus turn response. **Transfer:** transient line networks to clustered calligraphic trails. **Acceptance:** compare spatial queries against brute force; test ties, coincident agents, boundaries, traversal order, no-neighbor cases and update simultaneity; visually check startup, steady state and reset. Freeze the first narrow behavior before considering multiple species or collisions.

### C. Thick paths and reusable regions — next

**Artist task:** fill a surface with curved strips that respect neighboring strips and negative space. *Fidenza* is valuable because its stated non-overlap applies to thick shapes, not just centerlines. **Computation removed:** width-aware acceptance, caps/joins and valid strip boundaries. **Alternative:** current RK4 traces, resampling and offsets should compose the path; do not add a second tracer merely to bundle drawing.

**Inputs/output:** centerlines, width profiles, cap/join choice and occupied regions; return strip regions, acceptance/rejection information and retained paths. General Boolean operations and holes are a separate reusable dependency if required. **Edits:** width distribution, spacing, curvature and boundary margin. **Transfer:** river-like bands to typeset ribbons or woven-looking strips. **Acceptance:** crossing and near-tangent paths, endpoint collisions, sharp turns, closed routes, degeneracy and exclusion boundaries. A wider strip must actually change occupancy. No claims of weave topology or arbitrary polygon robustness without their own tests.

### D. Elastic curves and growth — after B

**Artist task:** make lines curl, crowd and grow into quilled forms; change growth distribution without drawing new paths by hand. Hodgin's *Individuation* supplies a clear distinguishing process. **Computation removed:** connected spring/bending constraints, neighborhood repulsion and controlled resampling/splitting. **Alternative:** smoothing or offsetting a finished line cannot produce its contact-driven evolution.

**Inputs/output:** nodes, edge connectivity, rest lengths, growth field, constraint parameters, timestep and budget; return new state with stable ancestry through splits. **Edits:** rest-length growth, bend preference, boundary shape and local growth bias. **Transfer:** quilling to folded coastlines or cell-like boundaries. **Acceptance:** straight-chain equilibrium, isolated bend, symmetric compression, resampling conservation and bounded growth; inspect short/long trajectories and crowded failures. Cap work and memory explicitly. First deliver 2D; surface growth, *Floraform* and Lomas's nutrient-driven cell division/reconnection remain separate open tasks even after that succeeds.

### E. Image-driven marks, washes and plotting — parallel after A/C as needed

**Artist task:** retain the image's tonal structure while replacing pixels with dots, stitches, hatch lines or translucent washes. **Computation removed:** select independently useful pieces: weighted sampling/centroid accumulation, region hatch generation, or recursively perturbed polygons with related variants. **Alternative:** color lookup, looping over marks and drawing a line remain ordinary p5 code; existing convolution supplies many gradient ingredients.

**Inputs/output:** owned raster/mask or region, sampling domain, explicit random stream and geometric controls; return positions, paths or polygon layers, not a frozen painted bitmap only. **Edits:** mark size versus density, hatch angle versus tone, edge deformation versus opacity. **Transfer:** portrait stipples to topographic hatching; watercolor washes to translucent cloud boundaries. **Acceptance:** source-image substitution, light/dark density response, small holes, clipping, fixed-area comparisons and layer-order checks. SVG/plot export is a bounded adapter with units and pen-up behavior, not a claim of machine-safe G-code. Split sampling, wash construction and plotting into separately reviewable slices.

### F. Browser materials, feedback and fluid simulation — staged

**Artist task:** animate retained image history, distort a texture, or carry dye through moving fluid. **Computation removed:** first a small explicit framebuffer lifecycle; then a selected solver if fluid is still the task. **Alternative:** use p5's existing framebuffers and shaders; do not build a general render-graph engine as a prerequisite.

**Inputs/output:** texture/state buffers, dimensions, density, boundary policy, uniforms and logical tick; return controlled state/texture handles within a p5 adapter, with clear ownership and disposal. A fluid core would separately expose velocity, pressure/divergence and transported scalar fields. **Edits:** feedback decay versus warp; fluid injection versus viscosity/obstacles. **Transfer:** distorted print textures to an interactive dye study. **Acceptance:** identity/no-feedback image, orientation, alpha, resize, reset, swap order and resource release; for fluids, transport of a known pattern and divergence reduction, then stability/convergence evidence. GPU output uses a declared profile and tolerance. A wave or curl demo must never substitute for pressure-projected flow.

### G. Typography, recorded signals and interaction — use existing browser primitives

**Artist task:** let words, speech, gestures or data change a composition while keeping the behavior editable. Davis's audio mappings, onformative typography and Levin/Lieberman's installations motivate different adapters. **Computation removed:** known-font contour extraction, recorded feature timelines and reusable mappings; only admit analysis algorithms when needed. **Alternative:** p5.Font and browser audio/camera infrastructure should be used before introducing a font engine or media framework.

**Inputs/output:** hashed font/assets, text and placement; or timestamped feature/event samples and explicit normalization; return contours, masks or time-indexed control values. **Edits:** replace the word without redesigning the mark system; move a frequency band from size to spacing. **Transfer:** audio logo to data-driven field, camera silhouette to cut-paper regions. **Acceptance:** deterministic prerecorded replay first, then separately tested live acquisition; missing assets, Unicode/font limits, permission lifecycle and timestamp gaps. Camera tracking/segmentation is a distinct algorithmic dependency. Do not promise the complete installations or general multilingual shaping from a font-points study.

### H. Real 3D presentation, then implicit geometry

**Artist task:** give existing geometry coherent depth, lighting, texture and shadows, then blend or carve forms that cannot be described by current mesh builders. **Computation removed:** first reusable mesh-to-p5 adaptation with explicit normals/UVs; later a bounded SDF/isosurface slice. **Alternative:** ordinary camera/material choices stay in examples; general solid modeling is too broad for one admission.

**Inputs/output:** indexed positions/faces, attribute arrays and mapping policy; then typed distance primitives/combination rules or a scalar grid, with a specified result as shader scene or extracted mesh. **Edits:** profile and material separately; blend radius, repetition spacing and cut shape. **Transfer:** Davis-like textured trajectories to independent product-like sculptures; implicit blobs to perforated shells. **Acceptance:** winding, seams, flat/smooth normals, texture orientation, degeneracies, silhouette and occlusion; SDF sign/scale fixtures, normals, bounded march termination and sampling resolution. Mesh extraction, raymarch rendering and fabrication are separate outputs with separate evidence.

### I. Specialized systems — bounded follow-on choices

Investigate space-colonization/venation, DLA, multiscale Turing fields, fractal flames, complex dynamics and map generation as distinct proposals. Each removes substantial work and has a clear visual reference, but they do not share one useful “organic generator” API. Require a named editable task, source mechanism, retained output and a second use before selection. For example, vein-network growth should preserve nodes/edges and expose attractor consumption; a multiscale pattern step should expose scales and competition state; a flame accumulator should expose transform probabilities and density/color accumulation. A city generator must distinguish road topology, embedded geometry, parcels and rendering.

Use controlled evidence for each: growth termination and node identity; scale competition versus ordinary Gray–Scott; flame density convergence and RNG order; route connectivity and parcel validity. Defer learned model training, distributed evolutionary services, full 3D cellular growth and production fabrication until their setup, dependencies and maintenance are justified by a separate user task. These are acknowledged exclusions, not claimed coverage.

## Consequences of the deeper collection audit

These findings extend the map without turning every series into a proposed API. The recommended first implementation remains A plus a narrow B. The following tasks require separate design decisions and distinguishable evidence before they enter a batch.

| Follow-on task | Computation and reusable output | Meaningful edit and transfer case | Evidence that would distinguish success |
|---|---|---|---|
| Let agents build the field they follow | Explicit deposit → diffuse/decay → sense → move schedule; detached grid and agent state | Deposit strength independently of sensor angle; transfer a branching trail system to self-organized transport patterns | No-deposit and no-sensing ablations, update-order tests, mass accounting under the chosen boundary rule, timed reset |
| Build actual interlacing | Crossing graph or stitch graph plus paths and spacing constraints | Replace a crossing pattern without replacing the renderer; transfer a planar weave to a curved panel | Over/under invariants, seam continuity, local clearance and changed stitch connectivity |
| Fold articulated panels | Hinge graph, panel transforms, angle constraints and explicit contact policy | Change a hinge angle or panel layout; transfer a screen-like surface to a compact folding study | Rigid panel edge preservation, matching hinge endpoints, collision reports and bounded iteration |
| Draw across identified boundaries | Domain mapping plus seam-consistent geometry and adjacency | Change periodic identification; transfer a repeating line drawing to wraparound puzzle adjacency | Crossing each seam preserves neighbor identity and avoids duplicate edges; keep physical puzzle-cut production outside scope |
| Evolve a river network | Centerline/bank state, migration rule, cut detection and persistent segment ancestry | Change erosion bias or cutoff distance; transfer river scars to evolving decorative channels | Topology changes only under declared triggers, stable bank widths, no invalid self-crossing output |
| Extract lines from 3D forms for plotting | Camera-projected slices, visible contour fragments and occlusion metadata | Change camera separately from stitch density; transfer an embroidery preview to pen plotting | Known hidden-edge fixtures and view-dependent silhouettes; sampling density must not reveal occluded segments |
| Grow a graph by geometric intersections | Stable node/edge IDs, split/link events and explicit relaxation | Change connection probability separately from relaxation strength; transfer tangled webs to restrained architectural networks | Crossing splits preserve incident edges and ancestry, no duplicate links, meaningful before/after connectivity |
| Draw a changing proximity graph | Relative-neighborhood construction, state update and node insertion | Change density-driven insertion independently of forces; transfer an elastic lattice to a spatial relationship drawing | Small brute-force oracle for neighborhood edges, deterministic ties, synchronous state and event order |
| Render a large point process clearly | Density/color accumulator and separate tone/reconstruction mapping | Change exposure without resimulating the point sequence; transfer nonlinear attractors to dense agent histories | Partitioned accumulation equivalence within stated arithmetic, zero-hit handling, convergence and saturation checks |

Cell division deserves its own future design: [Lomas’s Cellular Forms](https://andylomas.com/cellularForms.html) evolves nutrients, division and neighbor relationships. A retained cell graph with ancestry, editable division thresholds and lighting inputs could transfer from coral-like structures to vessel-like forms; fixtures must check division/reconnection and finite growth budgets. Refining an existing triangle mesh cannot substitute for this process. Corollaria’s flat-panel assembly is another distinct task: preserve panel geometry and seam adjacency, edit panel scale or assembly constraints, and validate paired seams and flattened distortion before discussing physical manufacture. Browser previews of paint, filament or multiple materials cannot establish the corresponding physical behavior.

The first two graph tasks are source-backed by Hoff’s [Tangle of Webs](https://inconvergent.net/2019/a-tangle-of-webs/) and [Differential Lattice](https://inconvergent.net/generative/differential-lattice/). Hoff describes normalized-vector relaxation rather than a physical Hookean spring model; substituting springs would be an independent design choice, not his documented mechanism. These tasks add topology changes to the proposed elastic-growth work; they should not be quietly implemented as example callbacks. [Sand Spline](https://inconvergent.net/generative/sand-spline/) provides a useful counterexample to visual algorithm guessing.

GenerateMe separates [log-density rendering](https://generateme.wordpress.com/2018/10/24/smooth-rendering-log-density-mapping/) from [vector-field composition](https://generateme.wordpress.com/2018/10/04/more-on-vector-fields-surfaces/). That separation is a promising reusable boundary: artists can change exposure and reconstruction without changing the system producing points. Sighack’s [value clustering](https://sighack.com/post/generative-value-sketches-using-k-means-clustering) and [random-walk flood fill](https://sighack.com/post/flood-fill-art-using-random-walks) motivate different state and region outputs despite both beginning with colored pixels. Exact simulation references and reviewed images are linked from the collection manifest and visual-review evidence.

The corpus is highly uneven by design: editions, generated variations, historical production stills and tutorial process figures all matter, but their frequency does not measure artist importance or implementation priority. A family with hundreds of McCabe or Reas variants does not receive hundreds of votes. Evaluate coverage by collections and mechanisms, then select work by reusable computation and artist benefit.

## Acceptance that tests useful capability

For every selected slice, write its artist task, computation removed, alternative, inputs/output/state, meaningful edit, transfer case and evidence limits in the capability decision before writing its operation contract. The corpus supplies motivation; it does not bypass semantic design or automatically become a conformance fixture.

Use three levels of evidence. Numerical/topological fixtures test the computation with distinguishing edge cases. An original editable native study tests that the artist no longer has to implement the missing algorithm. A separately declared structural or technique recreation tests the named reference, if enough source evidence exists. Keep exact rendering, structural/perceptual similarity and technique reproduction distinct. Never measure success solely by function count, image difference or resemblance from a thumbnail.

Before implementation, withhold a contrasting transfer reference from interface design. After the baseline works, substitute its field, mark, mask, motif or initial condition without modifying the algorithm. Review an edit that changes structure and an edit that changes appearance; determine explicitly whether they share randomness. Inspect temporal behavior at several logical ticks for stateful work, not just a final screenshot. Failure and unstable cases stay in the report.

Use the project's pinned **p5 2.3.2**, existing browser runners, package builders and shared native render lease. [Browser constraints](external-art-browser-constraints.md) records the verified substrate. p5 provides framebuffers, GLSL, custom geometry, font contour facilities and offscreen drawing, but their presence is not Procedurals support. The existing isolated layer runner uses Chromium with `--disable-gpu`; this is useful controlled evidence, not cross-driver validation. Audio is an addon/integration decision. Float textures, WebGL profiles, font assets, device density and timing need explicit scope. No numerical recommended ranges or performance claims are inferred from the reference images.
